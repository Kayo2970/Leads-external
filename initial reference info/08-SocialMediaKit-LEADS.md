## 1. Your Social Media Goal

Build credibility and visibility for LEADS as India's leading non-technical skill upliftment centre — driving awareness of events, website traffic, and direct enquiries from government, industry, founders, and students.

## 2. Platform Recommendations

| Platform | Why | Who Uses It | Content Type | Frequency | Time Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (Primary) | Reaches government, industry, and professional audiences directly — the natural home for a leadership institute | Professionals, executives, institutions | Event recaps, achievement posts, leadership insights, board/team spotlights | 3x/week | ~2–3 hrs/week |
| **Instagram** (Secondary) | Engages student and younger founder audiences with visual, behind-the-scenes content | Students, young professionals | Event photos/reels, quick highlights, quote graphics | 3x/week | ~2 hrs/week |
| **Google Business Profile** (Secondary) | Establishes local/national search presence and credibility | Anyone searching for LEADS or leadership centres near them | Event listings, photos, reviews | Ongoing (light maintenance) | ~30 min/week |

*Facebook and TikTok are not recommended given the primarily professional/institutional audience.*

## 5. Content Pillars

1. **Behind the Scenes** (always included) — Event setup, team moments, day-in-the-life at LEADS. Post ideas: (a) team prepping for an event, (b) a quick "what's happening this week at LEADS," (c) candid moment from a past summit.
2. **Event Spotlights** — Highlighting past and upcoming events. Post ideas: (a) Bharath Leadership Summit recap carousel, (b) Catalyst event teaser, (c) "This event changed how I think about leadership" attendee quote.
3. **Leadership Insights** — Short, credible takes connecting to the centre's mission. Post ideas: (a) "3 non-technical skills every founder needs," (b) board member perspective quote graphic, (c) stat card ("30+ events in Year 1 — here's what we learned").
4. **Community & Impact** — Showing the people and results behind the numbers. Post ideas: (a) attendee success story, (b) impact report highlight, (c) partner/institution shoutout.

## 6. Visual Layout Guide

- **LinkedIn post:** Square or 4:5 image, LEADS logo top-left corner, headline text in Royal Violet/Warm Gold overlay, body caption below the fold.
- **Instagram post:** 4:5 or square, consistent violet-gradient border/frame across all posts for grid cohesion; carousel format for event recaps (cover slide = event name in gold on violet).
- **Colour mapping:** Royal Violet (`#361B6A`) as the dominant background/overlay tone across all graphics; Warm Gold (`#D4A537`) for headlines, stats, and CTAs; white/off-white for body text.
- **Canva size reference:**
  | Asset | Dimensions |
  |---|---|
  | LinkedIn post | 1200x1200px |
  | LinkedIn cover | 1128x191px |
  | Instagram post | 1080x1350px (4:5) |
  | Instagram story | 1080x1920px |
- **Caption structure template:** Hook line (question or bold stat) → 2–3 sentences of context → CTA ("Read more on our website" / "Join us at the next event") → 3–5 relevant hashtags.

## 7. Competitor Social Snapshot

Based on sector research (Section 3 of the Competitor Brief), most comparable centres (IIM-A's leadership centre, IMT Hyderabad's centre) either have minimal social presence or post infrequently, institutional-style updates. LEADS' opportunity: post more consistently, with more visual energy and real event storytelling, to stand out as the more active, visible player in this space.

## 11. How to Measure Success

Track monthly: follower growth, direct enquiries/DMs, website traffic originating from social (via GA4), and posting consistency against the 3x/week target. At 30/60/90 days, "good" looks like: steady follower growth, at least a handful of qualified enquiries per month, and a visible uptick in social-referred website traffic.
