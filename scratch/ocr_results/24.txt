About The Event
LEADS Next Gen Centre representatives attended the BMA
symposium, "Al and the Future of Management Education &
Practice," addressing the gap between curricula and tech
disruption. The delegation joined discussions with dignitaries
including Prof. Vidyashankar S (VT), Mr. Ashwin Hegde Karkalc
(Cyware), and Sri Panka kumar Pandey, IAS.
Participation focused on identifying Al's corporate and
collaboration, and
defining the "human-in-the-loop" skills
recruiters demand. The event emphasized establishing Al as a
foundational literacy to prepare graduates for tech-driven
environments with ethical clarity.
