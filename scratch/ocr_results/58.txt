Valedictory Session - Opening
Address
Dr. Subhadeep Mukherjee opened the valedictory session by welcoming
dignitaries and reflecting on the summit's collective journey.
He emphasized that the Bharat LEAD Summit was more than an event it
was an emotional and transformational experience. He framed
multidisciplinary leadership aligned with the National Education Policy
as essential for future conversion.
Using the metaphor of seeds growing into trees, he surged students to
carry institutional values forward while serving both society and the
nation. He acknowledged the invisible efforts of faculty, volunteers, and
organizing committees whose coordination made
the summit's
ambitious scale possible.
Dr. Mukherjee closed by inspiring students to translate ambition into
execution across two demanding days.
Keynote Address: The New Age of
Leadership - Innovation, Technology,
and Nation Building
Mr. Nagana Gowda delivered a motivational keynote on
leadership, innovation, nation-building, and self-development.
He explained that meaningful transformation comes from
collective
engagement,
not
distant authority. The
keynote
covered the LEAD framework Leadership, Energize yourself and
others, Aspirational thinking, and Demonstrate action and
courage.
His powerful stories from Suhani Jalota's menstrual hygiene
initiative in Mumbai slums to Ankits work on food waste and
hunger highlighted the importance of social impact. He
concluded with a call for conscious,
courageous, and
responsible youth leadership rooted in discipline, awareness,
and respect for human life.
Report Reading, Journey Reflections &
Awards
Dr. Shilpa R. G., Assistant Professor, FMC, RUAS, presented the formal
summit report, followed by reflections on the LEADS Next Gen Centre's
journey from Mr. Gurutejas C., Senior President, and the summit
journey from Mr. Abhijit Arya, Senior Vice President.
Awards were presented to student leaders and contributors by Dr.
Kiran Kumar B. M., Associate Professor & Head (Peenya Campus).
The summit concluded with the National Anthem.
