About The Event
The technical sessions focused on frontier technologies, asset protection, and deep-tech commercialization.
Discussions highlighted the rapid adoption of Al across industries, demonstrating how deep learning, automation,
and digital twins are transforming healthcare, logistics, manufacturing, and PropTech. Speakers emphasized that
future entrepreneurs must combine domain expertise with strong data governance, multidisciplinary collaboration,
systems thinking, and responsible innovation to successfully scale emerging technologies.
Financial sessions addressed the funding challenges faced by
deep-tech startups due to long development cycles and high
capital requirements. Experts outlined a funding continuum
integrating government prototype grants, corporate venture
capital (CVC), angel investors, and procurement initiatives
such as Innovations for Defence Excellence (DEX), enabling
startups to secure early validation, reduce commercialization
risks, and accelerate market entry.
The conference concluded with a strategic roadmap for Viksit
Bharat 2047, where Shri Ramanan Ramanathan and Dr.
Anshuman Awasthi emphasized that India's deep-tech
ecosystem must strengthen collaboration between research
institutions, startups, and industry. They highlighted that
corporations should
innovation partners, enabling indigenous technologies to
scale successfully and contribute to long-term national
growth and self-reliance
