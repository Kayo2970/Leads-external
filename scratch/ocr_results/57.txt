Workshop: Leading Impact- Transforming
CSR in Strategic Leadership For
Sustainable Future
Ashwani Sinha's
session traced CS's evolution from charity-
based initiatives toward strategic business integration, arguing
that CSR is not mere philanthropy but a mechanism generating
social impact and business value simultaneously. Drawing on
RDx's telemedicine initiatives, he explained how healthcare
delivery reached approximately 1.3 Lakh people through rural and
specialist access. The
session explored three theoretical lenses:
profitability; Porter and Kramer's Shared Value Model, where
businesses solve social problems to generate growth; and C. K.
Prahalad's Bottom of the Pyramid concept, reframing low-income
communities as active customers. Practical examples included
rural diagnostics, GSK's shingles campaigns, and
Sankara Eye
Hospital's cross-subsidized model, proving that business and
society are fundamentally interdependent. The session touched
on the Companies Act 2013, citing Apollo Tyres and Bayer as
examples of CSR strengthening stakeholder ecosystems. Sinha
closed by challenging
students to integrate profitability and
ethical governance as one connected discipline.
