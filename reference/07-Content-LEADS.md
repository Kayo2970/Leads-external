## Cover Page
**LEADS Next Gen Centre** — Company Profile · Faculty of Management and Commerce, M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), Bengaluru

## Who We Are
LEADS Next Gen Centre is India's first dedicated centre for leadership and non-technical skill upliftment — built to develop the entrepreneurship, business, and leadership capabilities that complement India's strong technical education base.

## What We Do
We design and host events — from flagship national summits to focused workshops — that bring together government, industry, founders, students, and researchers to build real leadership and business capability.

## Our Story
Founded a year ago in Bengaluru, LEADS emerged from a simple observation: India produces exceptional technical talent, but leadership and non-technical business skills often lag behind. In just twelve months, LEADS has grown into a national platform, hosting more than 30 events including India's first Bharath Leadership Summit.

## By the Numbers
- 30+ events hosted in Year 1
- 1 flagship national summit launched (Bharath Leadership Summit)
- A growing national community spanning government, industry, academia, and student founders
