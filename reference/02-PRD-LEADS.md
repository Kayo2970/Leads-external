# Project Requirements Document (PRD)
**LEADS Next Gen Centre — Website** · v1.0 · Prepared by Kayomarz Pavri (LEADS Next Gen Centre)

---

## 1. Project Summary

LEADS Next Gen Centre needs a new website to introduce the centre, showcase its events and achievements, and convert visitors into leads. The site should feel premium and credible enough for government officials, industry leaders, and startup owners, while remaining engaging and accessible for students and researchers. Success means visitors clearly understand what LEADS does within seconds of arriving, can easily explore events and impact reports, and are consistently converted into contactable leads.

## 2. Goals

1. At least 20% of homepage visitors scroll to or interact with the Events section.
2. At least 10% of visitors who reach the Contact Us page submit the lead form.
3. Visitors can find and download an Impact Report in 2 clicks or fewer from any page.
4. The BLS (Bharath Leadership Summit) link and LEADS Portal link are used by visitors researching the centre's flagship programmes and future login access.
5. The site clearly communicates "India's first leadership and non-technical skill upliftment centre" as the core identity, measured by visitor survey/feedback post-launch.

## 3. Who Uses This Site

A broad, national audience: government officials, startup founders/owners, industry professionals, students, and researchers — essentially anyone in India seeking to develop leadership or non-technical (business, entrepreneurship) skills. They are visiting primarily to understand what LEADS' initiatives are, see proof of the centre's credibility and activity (events, achievements), and find a way to engage — whether that's attending an event, downloading a report, or getting in touch.

## 4. Pages Overview

| Page Name | What It Does | Who Sees It | Key Things on the Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Hero introduction to the centre; overview of mission and achievements | Everyone | Hero statement, key stats (e.g. 30+ events in Year 1), highlights of events/initiatives, calls to action |
| Events | Showcases all centre events | Everyone | Grid/list of events (e.g. Bharath Leadership Summit, Catalyst); clicking an event opens a pop-up with that event's details and branding — not a separate page |
| About Us | Tells the centre's story and credibility | Everyone | Mission, story, "by the numbers," **Leadership / Board of Members section**, affiliation with MSRUAS |
| Reports (Impact Reports) | Lets visitors download official reports | Everyone | Dropdown menu in navigation listing available PDF reports for direct download |
| Contact Us | Captures and qualifies leads | Everyone | Lead capture form (name, email, organisation, interest area, message) |
| *(Nav-only, external)* BLS | Sends visitors to the Bharath Leadership Summit's own website | Everyone | Opens in a new tab; brief on-hover context |
| *(Nav-only, external)* LEADS Portal | Sends visitors/members to the centre's ERP/login portal | Registered users (future) | Opens in a new tab; link goes live when the portal is deployed |

## 5. Must-Have Features

1. A working, validated lead-capture form on Contact Us that routes submissions somewhere the team can act on them (e.g. email inbox or spreadsheet, to be confirmed).
2. Pop-up event detail cards on the Events page — no dedicated URL per event, but each pop-up carries that event's own visual identity/logo.
3. A dropdown navigation item that lists and links out to downloadable Impact Report PDFs.
4. Two external navigation links (BLS, LEADS Portal) that clearly signal they leave the LEADS site.
5. Full responsiveness across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
6. Light and dark mode support.
7. A distinctive, animated hero background (the flowing "Threads" visual) that feels premium and alive without hurting performance.
8. A Leadership/Board of Members section within About Us.

## 6. Out of Scope (Version 1)

- Full LEADS Portal functionality (ERP/login system) — this is a separate, later project; the site only links out to it.
- Full BLS website functionality — LEADS' site only links to the existing BLS site.
- E-commerce, payments, or ticket sales.
- User accounts / gated content on the main marketing site.
- Multi-language support (assumed English-only for v1 — confirm if Hindi/regional language support is needed later).

## 7. How It Should Look and Feel

- **Tone:** Premium, professional, credible — never gimmicky, no emojis, no cluttered visuals.
- **Colours:** Royal Violet as the main brand colour, a softer supporting violet, and a warm gold accent for highlights and calls to action.
- **Background style:** A flowing, dynamic animated background on the homepage hero (inspired by the "Threads" visual style), calming down to a stiller version on interior pages, and fully static on the Contact form and Reports pages so nothing distracts from the task at hand.
- **Dark mode:** Included from day one, matching the same violet/gold identity in a deep, near-black violet palette.

## 8. Performance & Quality Standards

- Loads quickly on both desktop and mobile connections.
- Fully usable and legible on phones, tablets, and desktops.
- Readable by screen readers and usable via keyboard only.
- No broken links or dead-end pages.
- Animated elements never get in the way of reading or using the site, and automatically calm down for visitors who've asked their device to reduce motion.

## 9. Assumptions

1. The team (Kayomarz and colleagues) will build and maintain the site themselves; no outside agency is involved *(stated)*.
2. The site will initially run on a local/development environment before a public domain and hosting are finalised *(stated)*.
3. Lead form submissions will initially go to an email inbox or spreadsheet, to be confirmed by the team *(assumed)*.
4. Event pop-up artwork/logos exist or will be supplied per event; placeholders will be used where not yet available *(assumed, per Q28)*.
5. Impact Reports exist as, or will be produced as, downloadable PDF files *(assumed)*.
6. The reference site (msruas.ac.in) is used as a tone/credibility reference rather than a visual template to copy *(assumed)*.
